I am an applied economist with a Ph.D. in economics, concentrating on environmental and energy economics, and more than nine years of experience delivering economic analysis for governments, development finance institutions and private-sector clients. I am the founder and principal of [Regovia Ltd](https://www.regovia.co.uk), an independent economics consultancy focused on energy and power-sector economics, climate and environmental regulation, and economic policy appraisal. My work covers fuel costs and dispatch, tariff structures, financing and cost of capital, as well as carbon pricing and emissions trading systems. As a World Bank Group consultant, I have contributed to the 2021 and 2022 [State and Trends of Carbon Pricing](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/state-and-trends-of-carbon-pricing) reports, managed the [Carbon Pricing Dashboard](https://carbonpricingdashboard.worldbank.org/), and undertaken economic analysis of climate policies and environmental and energy taxation. Previously, I was a senior economist at McKinsey & Company, working on energy demand, climate risk and low-carbon investment.
